headline: "California youth sports coaches need CPR and AED certification by Jan. 1, 2027"
summary: "From Jan. 1, 2027, California youth sports groups must have CPR- and AED-certified coaches and a written cardiac emergency plan shared with parents every year (AB 310). From Jan. 1, 2028, every official practice and match needs an operational AED, and AB 387 (signed Sept. 27, 2026) says a field that has one must share it. Ask your league: who is certified, where is the plan, and where is the AED?"

Correction to the watch: AB 310 was signed Oct. 3, 2025, not Sept. 2026; only AB 387 was signed Sept. 27, 2026. The Jan. 1, 2027 coach CPR/AED and written-plan deadline comes from AB 310 (HSC 124238.5(b)); AB 387 restates (b) without a date and takes effect Jan. 1, 2027, so the deadline holds. Coaches must recertify at least every two years; training may be online or in person from an accredited organization. 'Shared with parents every year' compresses 'an annual electronic communication to the parents or guardians' that includes the plan or a link, AED locations, and the staff to notify. AED access for leagues and the AB 387 facility rules start Jan. 1, 2028. AB 387 does not require a facility to buy an AED (124238.5(e)); if the facility has none, the league must supply one for the entire practice or match. 'Youth sports organization' is the HSC 124235 definition: organizations, businesses, nonprofits, or local government agencies running amateur competitions, training, camps, or clubs for ages 17 and under.
