Your family has flights booked for December 27. Then the swim club posts its winter break schedule, and your swimmer's group is in the water at 7:30 AM every weekday until New Year's Eve.

Our [club swimming season calendar](/season-calendar/year-round-club-swimming/) calls December holiday training a club swimming tradition. Plan the swim schedule first and the trip second.

**The group sets the load.** Bergen Barracudas in New Jersey posted its [December 2026 winter break schedule](https://www.gomotionapp.com/team/njbbst/page/practices/winter-sc-practices) group by group. From December 28 through 31, its Elite group swims 6 to 9 AM and comes back at 6 PM for a second practice.

Its Pre-Comp, Red, and White groups get one hour a day, 9 to 10 AM. Same club, same week, and four and a half hours of water for one group against one hour for another.

**Some clubs push harder over break.** The Stingray Swim Team in Fairbanks, Alaska told families in [last December's newsletter](https://www.gomotionapp.com/aksst/UserFiles/File/2025-2026-q2-newsletter-121125%5F099653.pdf) that its Junior and Senior groups would increase their workload during holiday training. The club called it part of the plan for spring championship meets.

**Others shut the pool for days.** Saint Louis Tsunami's [2026-27 calendar](https://www.gomotionapp.com/team/ozslts/page/swim-groups/practice-schedule) has no practice December 23 to 27 or December 31 to January 3. On the days between, practice moves to 5 to 7 PM.

Dolphin Club in Virginia [keeps normal practice](https://www.gomotionapp.com/team/vadcst/page/swimmers/break-schedule-2025-2026) through December 23, then takes Christmas Eve and Christmas Day off. Back in the water December 27 to 30. Then it stops again for New Year's Eve and New Year's Day.

So the same holiday trip lands on a closed pool at one club and a full training week at another. Thanksgiving splits the same way: Tsunami takes November 25 to 29 off, while Dolphin Club runs a modified schedule the night before and skips only Thanksgiving Day.

**Ask what the coach wants while you are gone.** Stingray's coaches asked traveling swimmers to swim, or exercise in some form, while away. If that wasn't possible, the newsletter promised the group would "hit it hard after break and you'll be just fine."

One club's answer is not your club's answer. Email the coach for your swimmer's group, not the front office, before you pay for a flight.

Ask two questions. Which holiday practices matter most for this group, and what should your swimmer do on the days you are away?
