League Fridays feel settled by October. Kickoff time sits on the fridge magnet under next week's grocery list. You already know the parking lot, which student section is loudest, and which parent brings the snacks.

Then the regular season ends. Results pick the next opponent and the host site for kickoff. Your family calendar has to flex overnight after that update lands on every shared phone.

**Your state association owns the ladder.** NFHS writes the football playing rules most states adopt. That federation banner does not run one national high school football championship that every state feeds into.

Washington's WIAA and Oregon's OSAA each publish their own postseason maps. Other states use different round names, different week counts, and different final sites. Trust the PDF your athletic director posts.

**What the PNW calendar actually looks like.** Our [HS football fall calendar for the Pacific Northwest](/season-calendar/hs-football-fall-pnw/) marks the last regular-season game in week 1 of November and playoffs from week 2 onward.

For the 2026 Oregon season, OSAA's published windows put the regular season through October 31, first-round playoffs November 6-7, quarterfinals November 13-14, semifinals November 20-21, and state finals November 27-28.

Washington's 2026-27 WIAA football season lists first practice on August 19, 2026 and a season end of December 5, 2026. State semifinals land November 28, 2026 at various locations. State finals are December 4, 2026 for 1B, 1A, and 3A, and December 5, 2026 for 2B, 2A, and 4A, at Husky Stadium in Seattle.

Earlier Washington rounds follow the bracket WIAA posts through Tournament Central after seeding. Skip borrowed graphics. Pull the association page for your own classification instead of trusting a screenshot from another state.

**What changes on your Friday.** In league play you knew the road trip in August. In the bracket you find out the next site after the final whistle, or on the official bracket update that night.

Drives get longer. A twenty-minute league game becomes a three-hour trip, then an overnight if the next round sits across the mountains. Budget the weekend before the qualifier, not after the results PDF lands Sunday morning.

Tickets and pass gates tighten as rounds advance. WIAA's own football resources push pass-gate info, program info, and a "Know Before You Go" page once a team qualifies for state. Home bleachers in week 4 never matched a state gate line.

Streaming rules follow the host. Read the posted sheet for that round before you leave the driveway with a full car.

**Practice week is not two-a-days again.** [Two-a-days](/drive-there/two-a-days-what-parents-need-to-know/) belong to August camp: heat rules, helmets-to-pads progressions, and two sessions a day. Playoff weeks still include practice. They are not a second camp.

Protect sleep and schoolwork the same way you did in October. Adding private lessons on top of a playoff practice week is how bodies break in November.

**Pack for a bracket, not a homecoming.** The team email will list jersey colors and arrival time. It will not list the family kit that keeps a long Saturday from turning into a late Target run.

Borrow the useful parts of [packing for a tournament weekend](/team-parent/packing-for-tournament-weekend/): layers for a cold metal bleacher, a second pair of dry socks for your kid, snacks that survive a delay, cash or a card the school store actually takes, and a plan if the game goes long.

Keep an overnight bag half-ready. Packing at eleven at night after a loud win feels miserable every single time.

**When the season ends for most of the roster.** An elimination loss ends the school postseason that night. Full season. Not a failed one.

Only a thin slice of programs ever reach a state final weekend under the association bracket. Our calendar already says banquet planning starts while a few teams are still playing deep into December nights.

Same house. Different end dates, if you have kids on different teams.


If you are still sorting how deep the roster runs before these Fridays matter, [varsity versus JV in high school football](/drive-there/high-school-football-varsity-vs-jv/) is the clearer September conversation.

**The one question to ask this week.** Ask the coach which association document sets your classification bracket, when the next possible site is named, and where tickets or pass-gate rules will be posted. Get the link. Put every live date on the family calendar, then pack like the next Friday might not be at home.
