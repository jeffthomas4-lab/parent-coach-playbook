Lacrosse fall ball isn't one national program the way Little League baseball and softball are. Format and price depend on whether your kid's high school booster club, a USA Lacrosse-sanctioned club, or the town rec department is the one running it, and the tag swings by hundreds of dollars between them.

**When it happens.** Boys programs can open fall ball as early as August and run it into November. Girls fall ball is a shorter window: September and October clinics and play days, wrapped up by Halloween like the baseball and softball fall seasons already on this site.

**What's different on the field.** Contact rules don't relax because the season doesn't count. Boys still play under the same checking rule as spring: two hands on the stick, contact from the front or side, shoulders to waist only, nothing near the head or neck. Girls fall ball keeps the no-contact rules, goggles, mouthguard, no checking, same as March.

**Why September is a bad month for some boys.** September is also when the recruiting-track calendar recommends two full weeks off for kids who play spring high school ball and summer club lacrosse back to back. A player who spent June and July at recruiting tournaments walks straight into fall ball season with no break in between. The rest window exists because knees, shoulders, and lower backs take the beating all year, not because the sport is being cautious for no reason.

**What it costs.** Registration ranges from under fifty dollars at a rec league to several hundred at a club-run program, and no single number applies across enough of them to be worth quoting here. Girls fall ball specifically lands close to a tenth of what club lacrosse costs in the summer, $1,500 to $4,000, since there's no tournament travel attached to it. Check your own program's page before trusting a figure from anywhere else.

**Is it worth it.** A rec-level kid with room on the September calendar gets real value here: extra reps, a new position, nothing riding on the score. A club-track boy fresh off a heavy summer of showcases needs the rest more than another rep. For a girl, fall ball is the cheapest way in the door, no club price tag, no recruiting weight attached.

Fall ball isn't a season you owe your kid. Hand it to them if the calendar and the body both have room, not because everyone else's kid is playing.
