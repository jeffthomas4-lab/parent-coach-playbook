Your dancer said yes to the Nutcracker back in September. Now your sister wants to know if you are driving up for Thanksgiving, and the rehearsal sheet on the fridge shows a call on Saturday, November 28.

Our [studio ballet season calendar](/season-calendar/studio-ballet-nutcracker-year/) has a name for this month: the Nutcracker tax. Saturdays go to rehearsal, studio run-throughs start, and Thanksgiving weekend is in play.

**Every Saturday, plus weekdays for bigger roles.** Ballet Quad Cities rehearses its younger children's roles (party children, small mice, soldiers, angels) on Friday evenings and Saturday afternoons, per its [2026 audition page](http://www.balletquadcities.com/nutcracker-auditions). From November 2, roles like Clara, Fritz, and the large rats pick up weekday rehearsals from 3:00 to 4:15 PM, depending on school schedules.

Rehearsal slots are posted by role. A dancer cast in two roles answers both calls, so your family calendar is the sum of every role on her line of the cast list.

**Thanksgiving is a studio-by-studio call.** Three schedules published for 2026 give three different answers. [Dance It Up](https://www.danceitup.com/nutcracker/nutcracker-information) in North Grafton, Massachusetts rehearses its regular cast every Saturday, with Thanksgiving weekend included.

Ballet Quad Cities takes Friday, November 27 off and makes Saturday mandatory. The [Ballet Idaho handbook](https://balletidaho.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2026-Nutcracker-Handbook.pdf) skips Thanksgiving week, then calls the full cast to company rehearsals from 4:15 PM each weekday starting Monday, November 30.

And one more studio's [posted policy](https://www.civicdancecenter.com/nutcracker-rehearsals/) puts the pressure on the weekend before the holiday. Nobody gets excused from that Friday and Saturday. Miss them and the dancer does not perform.

**The absence math is small.** Ballet Idaho says missing more than one rehearsal puts participation at risk and can move a dancer to understudy. Ballet Quad Cities says two or more absences may cost the role, and Dance It Up allows two while asking families not to plan them, because kids get sick.

Use one on a trip and you have nothing left for a December fever.

**Ask before you book.** Ballet Idaho wanted every outside conflict reported by September 10, so a trip you add in November is already late there. Email the Nutcracker director or rehearsal coordinator before you pay for a flight or a hotel.

Ask two questions. Which Thanksgiving-week dates are mandatory for your dancer's roles, and does a missed date count against the absence limit?

**November bills, too.** Ballet Idaho's $120 production fee is due November 1. Dance It Up bills six required show tickets, $150 total, to each family's account on November 2.
