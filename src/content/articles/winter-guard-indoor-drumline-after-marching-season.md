The last marching show of the fall wraps in November. A week later your snare drummer comes home asking about indoor drumline, and the guard kid is still spinning a flag in the driveway.

Answer that question in November. The indoor season runs January through April, and its last regional weekends land right on top of concert band's March contests.

**What the two activities are.** Winter guard is indoor color guard: flags, sabers, mock rifles, and dance on a gym floor. Indoor percussion moves the battery and the front ensemble (the pit) inside.

Both run under [WGI Sport of the Arts](https://www.wgi.org/about/), the governing body for indoor color guard, percussion, and winds. Our [marching band season calendar](/season-calendar/hs-marching-band-fall/) puts the start of both seasons in January for crossover kids.

**School unit or independent unit.** WGI sorts every group into two divisions. A scholastic unit draws its members from one high school and the schools that feed it, so the band director is your contact.

An independent unit has no tie to a single school. Its rehearsal site, schedule, and fees come from the unit itself, so ask it directly.

WGI then splits units into classes. A Class is for beginning programs, Open is the middle, and World is the top.

**The 2027 calendar.** WGI's [color guard regionals](https://www.wgi.org/27cgevents/) run from February 6-7 to March 20-21, including Portland, Oregon on March 13-14. The [percussion regionals](https://www.wgi.org/27percevents/) start February 13-14 and also end March 20-21.

World Championships are in Dayton, Ohio: color guard April 8-10 and percussion April 15-17, per WGI's [future dates page](https://www.wgi.org/about/future-dates/).

If your unit plans to go, starting in 2027 every group that needs a hotel at Worlds has to book through the [official WGI Housing Service](https://www.wgi.org/travel/). Do not reserve your own room early and hope.

**March is the collision.** Our calendar puts solo and ensemble contests and the large-group concert festival in March. So do WGI's last regionals.

Ask both directors which March Saturdays they own before the audition, not after the schedule posts. A kid who already holds a concert chair (see [how chairs and sectionals work](/drive-home/band-chairs-and-sectionals/)) needs both answers on the family calendar.

**The November ask.** Get three things in writing before tryouts: rehearsal nights, competition weekends, and the fee. The [band pathway](/pathways/band/) maps the rest of the year.

Then circle the March Saturdays.
