November is when the score sheet starts. Our [all-star cheer calendar](/season-calendar/all-star-cheer-year-round/) puts the first competitions and travel weekends here, after October mocks and the family parent night.

If you already read [why October parent night is not the competition](/drive-there/october-cheer-parent-night-not-competition/), you know dress rehearsal from a judged meet. This piece maps the scored weekend clock that lands late.

**The schedule arrives in a rush.** Gyms and event producers post a detailed Order of Competition a few days before the event in many cases.

A rough block a week out is a draft. Do not book nonrefundable rooms against it.

Varsity Spirit's championship FAQ says the detailed order lists when the team reports backstage, warms up, and competes. Your gym forwards that file, or drops the times in Band, the moment those numbers stop moving.

**Four times, not one.** Parent chats talk like a single "comp time" exists. Count four labels instead.

Arrival or report time is when your athlete must stand at the venue fully ready: hair, makeup, uniform. One gym handbook pattern puts report 60 to 90 minutes before warm-ups. Another frames arrival about 90 minutes before performance, then a gather for stretch before coaches take the team.

Performance time is floor time. Grandparents screenshot that number. Leave the hotel before then, with margin for parking and security.

Awards time comes later. Plan for later.

**Warm-up is a closed room.** After gather, coaches walk the team into a timed warm-up or staging path. Parents stay outside.

Gym handbooks across the country write that rule in plain language. At major USASF and IASF events, warm-up entry stays limited to rostered athletes plus credentialed coaches or listed personnel. Do not treat the closed door as a personal snub.

Follow them into warm-up and the team can take a penalty. Sit, hold tickets, and stay reachable while they run the last pass on a clock that will not wait.

**Buy spectator tickets the way the producer sells them.** Varsity All Star brand events skip physical tickets and cash at the door in their published admissions rules. You need a card, a working email, and a digital ticket to scan.

Security lines stretch. Build buffer into arrival. Do not bank on five spare minutes before report.

Ticket prices change by event and year, so we refuse a fake national number here. Read the event page your gym links. Buy before you leave the hotel.

**Floor time is short. Venue time is long.** One gym's parent letter times novice routines near a minute and a half, prep near two minutes, and elite near two and a half. Your division's music length follows that event's rules, yet the shape stays familiar: the floor pass is a blip.

You can still spend most of a workday inside the building. Awards sit on the Order of Competition or block schedule, and athletes return in full uniform with phones away for that ceremony in standard gym guidance.

At the biggest end-of-season meets, awards blocks land hours after a division finishes. Local November openers run shorter. The wait still beats the routine length either way, every time.

Pack snacks that will not wreck a stomach before a second run if your team has one.

**Hotel nights follow the day count.** For many one-day Saturday meets, families book Friday night because morning windows start early and venues open near 8 a.m. Two-day events add Saturday night. Expect a late Sunday drive home when your division sits at the back of the order.

Some nationals later in the season require producer hotel blocks through a travel partner. Your first November weekend is simpler gym travel. Still refuse to book rooms against a fantasy 2 p.m. performance time from an October group chat.

Once the Order of Competition posts, lock the hotel to the real report time plus traffic. Hope is not a checkout plan.

**What this weekend is not.** Skip rebuilding the packing list here. Use our [cheer competition packing list](/drive-there/cheer-competition-packing-list/) for the bag, the bow case, and the school ID.

Skip the annual budget lecture too. Competition fees and travel already live inside [how much competitive cheer costs](/drive-there/how-much-does-competitive-cheer-cost/), and this weekend is the first time those line items turn into a clock you can miss.

**The one printout to make.** When the Order of Competition lands, write four lines: arrival or report, gather if listed, performance, awards. Add the venue door and the warm-up handoff spot your coach names.

Pack the night before, buy the digital ticket, and leave early enough that a security line stays boring.

The score sheet starts in November. Your calendar should start the moment the schedule finally arrives.
