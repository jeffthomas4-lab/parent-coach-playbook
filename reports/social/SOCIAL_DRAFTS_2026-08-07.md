## 1. Buying a softball glove this month? Don't buy for next year.

**Source:** newest published, 2026-08-05 — https://parentcoachdesk.com/game/best-youth-softball-gloves/
**Format:** checklist / list post

**Core copy:**
> Buying a softball glove this month? Don't buy for next year's kid. Buy for the one playing right now.
>
> A glove with growing room teaches two-hand squeezing and slows the catch down. Size for the season in front of you.
>
> - Tee-ball / 6U: 9"-10"
> - 8U: 10"-10.5"
> - 10U: 10.5"-11"
> - 12U: 11"-11.5", position starts to matter here
> - 14U and up: 11.5"-13"
>
> Budget $25-$40 for tee ball, $40-$80 for a synthetic glove at 8U-10U. New leather needs a break-in: conditioner, a ball worked into the pocket, a rubber band holding it shut overnight, three to five days running.
>
> Plan to replace it every one to two seasons at these ages. Their hand outgrows the glove faster than the glove wears out.

## 2. The shin guard test isn't your kid's age. It's their height.

**Source:** newest published, 2026-08-04 — https://parentcoachdesk.com/game/best-kids-shin-guards/
**Format:** tip card

**Core copy:**
> The shin guard test isn't your kid's age. It's their height.
>
> Under 3'6": toddler/XS, 4-5". 3'6" to 4'0": youth XS/S, 5-6". 4'0" to 4'6": S/M, 6-7". 4'6" to 5'0": M/L, 7-8". 5'0" to 5'6": L/XL, 8-9".
>
> Under FIFA Law 4, a shin guard has to sit under the sock and provide what the rule calls "reasonable protection." A guard that's the wrong size for your kid's height doesn't clear that bar, even if it's technically being worn.
>
> A lower-leg impact without a properly fitted guard can sideline a kid anywhere from two days to four weeks. Fit is the job here. The brand isn't.

## 6. What two-a-days actually looks like, and the heat rule that matters most

**Source:** evergreen resurface, originally published 2026-06-11 — https://parentcoachdesk.com/drive-there/two-a-days-what-parents-need-to-know/
**Format:** link post

**Core copy:**
> Two-a-days means two practices a day, three to four hours apart, 90 minutes to two hours each. Most states require four to five days of shells-or-helmets-only before a team can move to full pads and real contact.
>
> Exertional heat stroke is the leading cause of preventable death in high school athletics, and August two-a-days are peak risk. Know the difference: heat exhaustion looks like heavy sweating, nausea, cramps, and feeling lightheaded. Heat stroke looks like hot skin, confusion, and a kid who's stopped sweating.
>
> If you see heat stroke signs, that's a 911 call. Not a drive to urgent care.
>
> Sleep floor during camp week is 8 to 9 hours. Send them to bed early, even when they push back.

**Source:** seasonal (SEASONAL_PLAN_2026-08.md social queue, band — camp is happening in real time), originally published 2026-05-12 — https://parentcoachdesk.com/drive-there/band-marching-band-camp/

**Core copy:**
> Two water bottles, 64 ounces minimum for the day, more in real heat. A hat with an actual brim, not a backwards cap. Stick sunscreen, SPF 50, because she'll actually reapply that kind. Broken-in shoes, bought at least two weeks early if she needs new ones.

## 8. Hockey gear: what to buy new, what to buy used, and what it should cost

**Source:** seasonal (SEASONAL_PLAN_2026-08.md social queue, hockey — pairs with the tryout-prep content already surfaced this month), originally published 2026-06-11 — https://parentcoachdesk.com/drive-there/youth-hockey-equipment-guide-parents/
**Format:** checklist / list post

**Core copy:**
> Buy new, no exceptions: helmet with cage, jock/jill, neck guard, mouthguard. Helmets degrade after impact even with no visible damage, so a used one isn't a real savings.
>
> Buy used, no problem: shoulder pads, elbow pads, gloves, pants, and the bag, as long as the padding is intact and the straps still hold.
>
> Fit matters more than price on two items: skates should be snug at the heel with about a quarter-inch of toe room, and shin guards should run from the skate boot to just below the kneecap.
>
> Budget $300 to $600 for a first-year setup if you shop the used categories. Most rinks rent full gear for kids under eight, if you want to test the commitment before buying anything.
