## 2. Thu discussion: losing streak car ride scripts

**Source:** published 2026-10-07 - https://parentcoachdesk.com/team-parent/losing-streak-youth-team/
**Format:** discussion / script post

**Core copy:**
> A losing streak gets worse when the car ride turns into a film session. Let them decompress first.
>
> Ages 7-9: offer a snack or quiet. Ages 10-12: one thing to work on next practice. Ages 13-17: you are here when they want to talk, no pressure.
>
> Track one or two controllable behaviors for a few matches instead of watching the scoreboard.
>
> Scripts and a short practice plan: https://parentcoachdesk.com/team-parent/losing-streak-youth-team/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=2026-10-07

## 3. Fri newsletter echo: recovery talk at 13-14

**Source:** safety refresh live - https://parentcoachdesk.com/drive-home/the-recovery-talk-at-13-14/
**Format:** tip card / newsletter echo

**Core copy:**
> They are sore after travel ball. Stretching still sounds optional. Sleep is the lever.
>
> The American Academy of Sleep Medicine says teens 13 to 18 need 8 to 10 hours regularly. The CDC cites the same range. Pair that with a short post-practice stretch habit and stop before it turns into a lecture.
