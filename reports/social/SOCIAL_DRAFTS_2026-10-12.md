## 2. Practice checklist: A 55-minute U12 basketball practice

**Source:** https://parentcoachdesk.com/team-parent/basketball-practice-plan-u12/
**Format:** checklist / practice plan

**Core copy:**
> Eleven fifth graders, one hoop, and an hour. Skip the layup line.
>
> Run four short blocks: 10 minutes of ball handling, 20 on one skill, 12 of stations and small games, then 8 to 10 of scrimmage. No drill runs past about 11 minutes, because kids this age focus for roughly one minute per year of age.
>
> USA Basketball sets practice at 45 to 75 minutes, twice a week, for ages 9 to 11.
>
> The full minute-by-minute plan: https://parentcoachdesk.com/team-parent/basketball-practice-plan-u12/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=2026-10-12

## 3. Tip card: What an ECNL showcase weekend is

**Source:** https://parentcoachdesk.com/drive-there/ecnl-showcase-what-it-actually-is/
**Format:** tip card / explainer

**Core copy:**
> An ECNL fall weekend is not a tryout for your kid.
>
> It is club games with college coaches on the sideline, taking notes. Your kid plays for their club team in real league matches, the same way they would on any other weekend. Nobody runs drills.
>
> So keep it normal: sleep, food, warm-up.
