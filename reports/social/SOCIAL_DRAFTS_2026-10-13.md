> Halftime snack duty, the five-minute version.
>
> Water and a piece of fruit cover most halftimes. Orange slices and banana halves digest fast and fit in one sweaty hand.

## 4. Fri link post: before you switch club volleyball teams

**Source:** Switching Youth Volleyball Teams: A Parent's 6-12 Week Plan (published 2026-10-12, rewritten 2026-10-12 (dek and bluf fixed)) - https://parentcoachdesk.com/game/switching-teams-youth-sports/
**Format:** link post / script (Fri)

**Core copy:**
> Thinking about switching club volleyball teams? Check one thing before you call a new club.
>
> Ask your current club's registrar, in writing, whether a signed commitment ties your athlete to the team this season. In USA Volleyball's Southern California region, the junior girls rules make a signed commitment letter binding.
>
> One rough month is not a pattern. A kid who stops wanting to go is one. So is a coach problem that one calm, respectful conversation with the coach did not fix.
>
> If you do leave, keep the exit short: "We've appreciated the season, but this isn't the right fit for us going forward."
