## 1. 2026-10-16: confidence-after-bad-game

**Source:** confidence-after-bad-game - https://parentcoachdesk.com/drive-home/confidence-after-bad-game/
**Format:** script / what to say (Thu catch-up)

**Core copy:**
> A bad game does not have to wreck the week.
> 
> In the first hour, keep it light. Offer water or a hug, and let your athlete choose whether to talk.
> 
> Then help them pick one process goal for the next practice. Confidence is a skill you can train, the same way USOPC mental performance guidance frames it.
> 
> If sleep and mood are off, fix those before you add extra drills.

> Snack schedule, picture day, carpool threads, and tournament hotels are different jobs. Mixing them without a handoff is how burnout starts mid-season.
