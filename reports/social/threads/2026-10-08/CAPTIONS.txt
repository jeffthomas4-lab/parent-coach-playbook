School owns some nights. Say which ones before the club builds the week around you.

Write the non-negotiables once: band concerts, choir dress rehearsals, religious school, family care nights. Send them to the team parent and the coach in the same note.

Protect sleep on school nights. A late optional clinic is easier to skip when the calendar was honest in week one.

Nutcracker week runs on parent jobs nobody lists on the registration form.

Costume steamer, quick-change helper, lobby greeter, cast-snack lead. Pick one lane early so you are not improvising in the wings.

Ask the stage manager which jobs need a quiet adult versus a strong back. Then put your name on one and stop.

October parent night is not the scored weekend. Treat it like a dress rehearsal for your family calendar.

Watch how long warm-ups run, where parents sit, and what the coaches actually want from the crowd. Take notes for November travel weekends.

Skip the scoreboard talk in the car. Ask what felt solid and what they want to clean up next practice.
