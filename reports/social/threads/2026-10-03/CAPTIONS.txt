School just added a team on top of club. A short note can turn two full calendars into one week everyone can see.

Name the nights school keeps, the club nights you hope to keep, and one weekday off organized sport. Ask the club to confirm that week, or to suggest a swap. One sport in a day, and one day off organized sports. The sample in the piece is a shape. Your dates come from the school email.
