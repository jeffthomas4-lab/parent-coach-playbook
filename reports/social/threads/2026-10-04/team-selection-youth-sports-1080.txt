Team selection fights start when families guess the rules. Publish the purpose and the scoring before anyone steps on the field.

Write a short purpose statement: competitive, developmental, or recreational. Score skill, effort, and coachability on a 1 to 5 scale, with at least two observers at each station.

Keep the cards. Those numbers answer the hard parent email later.

Results go out on a timeline you already shared. One follow-up conversation per family, then the window closes.
