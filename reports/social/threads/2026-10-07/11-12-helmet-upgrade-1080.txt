At 11 or 12, check fit before you buy a new helmet. A helmet that still fits can stay in play if the recert path is real.

NOCSAE certification holds when a licensed reconditioner follows the maker's schedule. A sporting-goods counter swapping foam is not that. NAERA members will not recertify football helmets 10 years or older from the first season of use.
