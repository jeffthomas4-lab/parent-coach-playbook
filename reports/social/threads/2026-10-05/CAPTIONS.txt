Team playing-time fights start when the rules stay in someone's head. Put the policy in writing before the first game.

Younger teams: equal rotation or a guaranteed-minute floor. Older competitive teams: earned time with clear criteria. Share it at a preseason meeting, then follow athlete to coach to parent when questions come up.

Use the 24-hour rule after a tough game. Ask for specific next steps, not fairness complaints.
