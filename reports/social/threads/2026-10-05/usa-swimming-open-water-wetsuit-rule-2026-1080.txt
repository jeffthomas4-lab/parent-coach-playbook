USA Swimming just opened local open-water meets to wetsuits that meet World Aquatics rule 6.9 even if the suit is missing from the approved list.

R-6 passed Sept. 26, 2026, and it is effective now. Zone Championships and national-qualifying meets still require a listed wetsuit.
