# Source pack: nutcracker-november-weekends-thanksgiving

**Date:** 2026-10-13
**Agent:** Ed

## Primary sources

1. PCD season calendar `src/content/seasonCalendars/studio-ballet-nutcracker-year.md`
   - November: rehearsal load climbs to full weekends; studio run-throughs; Thanksgiving weekend rehearsals at many companies.
   - Note: "November is the Nutcracker tax. Saturdays and Sundays go to rehearsal, Thanksgiving travel needs the studio's blessing, and a dancer in multiple casts is at every call."
2. Ballet Quad Cities, Nutcracker Auditions (2026): http://www.balletquadcities.com/nutcracker-auditions (fetched 2026-10-13)
   - Younger children's roles (Party Children, Small Mice, Soldiers, Angels, Cookies) rehearse Friday evenings and Saturday afternoons, one to two times per week.
   - Clara, Fritz, Large Rats, Chinese, and Ukrainian "may be called for weekday rehearsals beginning November 2 from 3:00-4:15 PM, depending on school schedules."
   - "Two or more absences may result in loss of role."
   - "Please list all rehearsal conflicts ... on the audition registration form."
   - Important dates: "November 27 No rehearsals - day after Thanksgiving"; "November 28 Mandatory Nutcracker rehearsal".
3. Dance It Up (North Grafton, MA), 2026 Nutcracker Information: https://www.danceitup.com/nutcracker/nutcracker-information (fetched 2026-10-13)
   - "Regular cast: every Saturday, including Columbus, Veterans, and Thanksgiving weekends."
   - "We allow 2 absences maximum per student, after which they may be removed from a role ... please do not 'plan' for two weeks off."
   - "Each Nutcracker family is required to purchase 6 tickets ($25.00 each) ... The six tickets ($150.00) will be charged to your account on November 2."
   - Note: the production is billed "Not Just Nutcracker" and mixes genres; it is still the studio's Nutcracker production.
4. Ballet Idaho Academy, 2026 Nutcracker Handbook (PDF): https://balletidaho.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2026-Nutcracker-Handbook.pdf (downloaded and read 2026-10-13)
   - Studio rehearsals "EVERY Saturday (except Thanksgiving week) through production", full cast Monday-Saturday "starting the end of November".
   - Mandatory company rehearsals "Nov. 30 - Dec. 4, 4:15 pm and later".
   - Conflicts due "NO LATER THAN THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 10. Missing more than one rehearsal will jeopardize student participation and the student may be cast as an understudy."
   - "A production fee of $120 for all cast members is due November 1, 2026."
5. Civic Dance Center, "Nutcracker Rehearsals" post: https://www.civicdancecenter.com/nutcracker-rehearsals/ (fetched 2026-10-13; post shows "13 Nov" with no year, so the piece cites the policy without its dates)
   - "WEEKEND BEFORE THANKSGIVING ... NO ONE WILL BE EXCUSED FROM THOSE REHEARSALS."
   - "If your dancer is unable to attend rehearsal on any of the following dates, they will be unable to perform in the production."
