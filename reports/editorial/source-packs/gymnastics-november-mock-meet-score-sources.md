## Primary sources

1. USA Gymnastics, Women's Development Program: https://usagym.org/women/development/ (fetched 2026-10-09)
   - "They must advance one level at a time by scoring the minimum mobility score at any USA Gymnastics sanctioned competition (Exception: Levels 1-3 do not have a mobility score)."
   - Levels 4 and 5 are the compulsory segment; competitive opportunities up to and including State Championships.
   - Competitive year is August 1 through July 31.
2. USA Gymnastics, Women's Rules and Policies index: https://usagym.org/women/rules/ (fetched 2026-10-09)
   - Hosts the 2026-2027 Rules and Policies (Development, Xcel) and the 2026-2027 Development Program Update.
3. 2026-27 entry and mobility chart (Rules and Policies), as summarized by GymnastGem (https://gymnastgem.com/mobility-scores/) and the CT NAWGJ 2026-2027 Development Program Update (https://www.ctnawgj.org/_files/ugd/bd942c_6dff82696e3e4a0f875ff0fc236cba43.pdf):
   - Level 4 to Level 5: 34.00 AA earned at Level 4.
   - Level 5 to Level 6: 32.00 AA earned at Level 5.
   - Two 36.00 AA scores at Level 4 (current or previous season) to skip Level 5.
   - Mobility AA is one meet's four-event total; scores from different meets do not combine.
4. PCD season calendar `src/content/seasonCalendars/year-round-gymnastics.md`
   - Nov: mock meets, final preparation. Jan: first meet of the season.
