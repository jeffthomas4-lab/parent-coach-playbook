| Claim | Source | Notes |
|-------|--------|-------|

## Warm-up / backstage access

| Claim | Source | Notes |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Parents are not allowed backstage / in warm-up; only credentialed coaches | Zia Cheer; UMAS handbook; [Panthers All Star Cheer 2024-25 handbook](http://panthersallstarcheerleading.com/images/2024-25_PAC_Handbook.pdf); [Peach Pit 2026-27 handbook](https://www.peachpitgym.com/_files/ugd/3627e0_b70ded363f244a8cafbbecfb3d9ce260.pdf) | Consistent gym policy pattern |
| At major USASF/IASF events, warm-up entry is limited to rostered athletes and listed coaches/personnel with credentials | [IASF / Worlds coach materials](https://www.iasfworlds.net/worlds/the-2026-cheerleading-dance-worlds/); Worlds Coach Hub | Elite-event credential standard; first local weekend still follows parents-out-of-warm-up |
| Timed warm-up blocks appear on official performance orders (example: 6-minute stretch/tumble + 12-minute floor warm-up columns ahead of competition time) | [Cheerleading Worlds 2026 competition times PDF](https://cheertheory.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/26-Worlds-Cheer-Order-4.21.pdf) | Illustrates why performance time is not arrival; regional events use their own Order of Competition |
