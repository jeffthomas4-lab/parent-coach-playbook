## Region / governing guidance on clinics vs tryouts

| Claim | Source | Notes |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Open gym sessions or instructional clinics are usually not considered part of the tryout process; most clubs do not require athletes to attend these pre-tryout events to attend tryouts | [Carolina Region Athlete and Club Bill of Rights for Tryouts (rev 2022 PDF)](https://carolinaregionvb.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/JuniorBillofRightsrev2022.pdf) | Document states principles are guidance encouraged for clubs, not absolute Region policy; phrase as Bill of Rights language / consumer guidance |
| Clubs may charge a tryout fee; multi-session tryouts may cost more | Same Carolina Bill of Rights | Safe for "tryout fees are real and separate" |
| Athletes should have chances to try out with multiple clubs; pressure to commit before the club's announced commitment date violates the Bill of Rights ideals | Same | Use for sales-pitch / early-pressure framing |
| AZ Region Open House Period: club may not require attendance at more than one Open House; may not offer positions, collect commitment fees/down payments, or ask for commitment prior to tryouts during Open House | [Arizona Region girls tryouts page](https://www.azregionvolleyball.org/girls-tryouts) | Example of a region with explicit open-house limits; tell parents to read their own region page |
| Region tryout calendars and commitment rules are set by the Region (example: SCSN posts division-specific tryout open dates and Letter of Commitment timing for 2026–27) | [USAV SCSN Junior Girls tryouts](https://usavolleyball.org/scsn/scsn-junior-girls/) | Proves tryout windows are Region-dated; do not invent a single national November-only rule for every Region |
