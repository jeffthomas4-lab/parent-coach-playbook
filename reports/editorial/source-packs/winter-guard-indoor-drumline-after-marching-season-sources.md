**Date:** 2026-10-06

## Primary sources

1. WGI Sport of the Arts, About: https://www.wgi.org/about/ (fetched 2026-10-06)
   - Governing body for indoor color guard, percussion, and winds.
   - Winter guard = indoor color guard: flags, sabers, mock rifles, dance and movement.
   - Indoor percussion = marching percussion (battery) plus front ensemble (pit).
   - Divisions: Scholastic (members from the same high school or its feeder schools) and Independent (not necessarily tied to a school).
   - Classes: A (beginning), Open (intermediate), World (most advanced).
2. WGI 2027 Color Guard Calendar: https://www.wgi.org/27cgevents/ (fetched 2026-10-06)
   - Regionals February 6-7 through March 20-21, 2027; Portland, OR March 13-14.
   - Color Guard World Championships April 8-10, 2027.
3. WGI 2027 Percussion Calendar: https://www.wgi.org/27percevents/ (fetched 2026-10-06)
   - Regionals February 13-14 through March 20-21, 2027 (Portland, OR March 20-21).
   - Percussion World Championships April 15-17, 2027.
4. WGI Future Dates: https://www.wgi.org/about/future-dates/ (Dayton, OH; 2027 dates above).
5. WGI World Championships Travel: https://www.wgi.org/travel/
   - "Beginning with the 2027 WGI World Championships, all groups wishing to participate and needing to secure overnight hotel accommodations must utilize the official WGI Housing Service."
6. PCD season calendar `src/content/seasonCalendars/hs-marching-band-fall.md`
   - Nov: marching season ends; concert band auditions and chair placements.
   - Jan: winter guard and indoor percussion begin for crossover kids.
   - Mar: solo and ensemble contests; large-group concert festival.
