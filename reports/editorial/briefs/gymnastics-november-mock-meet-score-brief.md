## Angle

Gymnastics coverage on the site has levels, meet-day logistics, packing, the drive home after a fall, costs, and recruiting. Nothing covers the November mock meet, the first score a Development Program gymnast sees each year. The parent-side gap: that score does not count toward mobility, because USA Gymnastics only counts mobility scores from sanctioned competitions. Use the mock meet as a dress rehearsal and a fix list for January.
