## Angle

Parents whose child already has a club schedule are about to add middle school sports. The piece gives one move: the parent writes the capped week and emails the club, instead of asking the club what works. Scripts for the club, the child, and the household. Not a lecture on specialization.

## Must say

1. Parent sends the week. Do not open by asking the club to design it.
2. Sample note is a shape. Dates and times come from the family's own school email.
3. AAP 2024 clinical report: no more than one sport in a day, at least one day off organized sports each week, overlapping schedules must not erase that rest day, two to three months off each sport per year. Guidance, not a diagnosis and not an injury guarantee.
4. Optional club hours and school open gyms still count as organized sport.
5. If the club requires the old full week plus school, drop a night or drop a team. The child does not negotiate that.

## Must not say

- Invented studies, prices, or a national school start date.
- Real family, player, or coach names.
- Public use of a founder's name.
- Medical advice.
