## Must say

1. PCD all-star calendar: November opens first competitions and travel weekends after October mocks/parent night.
2. Exact performance times often land only a few days before the event (gym + producer pattern).
3. Arrival / report / gather time is not performance time; athletes are ready hours earlier.
4. Warm-up / backstage is for athletes and credentialed coaches; parents stay out (gym handbooks + producer FAQs).
5. Spectator admission at many Varsity All Star events is digital ticket / card, not cash at the door.
6. Elite-style routine length is about 2.5 minutes; the family day is much longer, with awards often hours after the floor time.
7. One-day vs multi-day events change hotel nights; book to the gym's travel guidance once the schedule posts.
8. Link packing list for the bag; do not rehash packing.
