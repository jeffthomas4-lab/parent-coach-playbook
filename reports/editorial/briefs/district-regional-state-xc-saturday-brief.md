## Angle

Existing XC coverage owns August time trials, first-season primers, recruiting, and varsity odds. Nothing walks a parent through what changes when the Saturday meet stops being an invitational open field and becomes a qualifier with a named round (district / regional / state) and a cut list.

The gap: logistics and who still races, not training advice.

## Must say

1. NFHS writes race rules; each state association builds its own postseason ladder and labels.
2. Names and steps vary (OH district→regional→state; OR league/special district→state; WA district→state; KY regional→state). Do not invent a universal bracket.
3. Teams and individuals can advance on different paths.
4. Saturday changes: travel radius, race-call times, spectator rules, fewer races to watch, season-end for kids who do not advance.
5. Postseason open races (Nike Cross, Foot Locker regionals) are a separate thin slice after school state.

## Must not say

- Invented statewide qualifier counts as if they were national.
- Medical advice, recruiting predictions, or "national champion" framing for HS.
- Public Jeff Thomas naming.
