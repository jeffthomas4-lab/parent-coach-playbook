Our season calendar already names December as choir's playoff month: three to six performances in three weeks, each with a call time ahead of the listed start. Existing choir articles own all-state auditions, fall dress code, solo and ensemble, tours, and foreign-language repertoire. The gap: a November planning piece that turns "holiday concert season" into a dated list of call times, tech/dress rehearsals, community gigs, and conflicts with other kids' sports.

## Must say

1. PCD choir calendar: December is peak; 3–6 performances in three weeks is a normal active-program pattern; each has a call time well before the listed start.
2. November is when holiday repertoire and extra sectionals show up — that is the planning window.
3. Map every dated ask: winter concert, community/assembly gigs, caroling, tech and dress rehearsals.
4. Call time is not start time; published parent letters often put report ~30–90 minutes early, sometimes with daytime tech blocks the day before.
5. Multi-kid households: December choir collides with other sports' travel and holiday plans; put choir on the shared calendar first.
6. Required-performance / grade language appears in some school letters — check your director's policy, do not invent a national rule.
7. Link dress-code and foreign-language pieces instead of rehashing them.
8. End on an action: print the December list before Thanksgiving travel is booked.
