## Must say

1. PCD club volleyball 14s calendar puts pre-tryout skill clinics in October and USAV-regulated club tryouts in November.
2. Region and club guidance treats instructional clinics / open gyms as separate from the tryout process in many places; Carolina Region Bill of Rights language says open gyms or clinics are usually not part of tryouts and most clubs do not require them to attend tryouts (guidance document, not hard USAV statute).
3. Arizona Region open-house rules (example of a stricter region): clubs may not require attendance at more than one open house, may not make offers or collect commitment fees during that window.
4. Club FAQ pattern: clinics labeled "not required" even when "highly recommended" (cite as club practice pattern, not national rule).
5. Parent decision frame: clinics buy familiarity and reps; they do not buy a roster spot. Paying three clinics plus three tryout fees can stack before any offer arrives.
6. Sales-pitch signals: claiming clinic attendance is required for a fair look when region/club copy says otherwise; pressure to commit before the published tryout window; bundling clinic fees as if they were the tryout.
