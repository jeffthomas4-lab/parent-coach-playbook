## Must say

1. NFHS writes the playing rules most states adopt; each state association builds its own playoff ladder and championship. There is no single NFHS national high school football champion bracket.
2. PNW timing from our calendar plus cited WA/OR dates: regular season ends early November; Oregon OSAA first round Nov 6-7 2026 through finals Nov 27-28; Washington WIAA state semis Nov 28 2026 and finals Dec 4-5 2026 at Husky Stadium. Earlier WA rounds follow the published bracket after seeding, not inventable here.
3. What changes for families: unknown next site until the result is in, longer drives and possible overnight stays, tighter tickets/pass gates at later rounds, season can end any elimination game.
4. Two-a-days are August. Playoff weeks still have practice, but they are not a second camp.
5. Other states differ. Read your state association PDF, not a national graphic in the group chat.
