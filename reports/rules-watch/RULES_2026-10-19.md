## Result: one new parent-actionable change (California), drafted

### California AB 708: leagues must allow soft-shell helmet covers

- Signed Sept. 28, 2026 (Chapter 732; adds Health and Safety Code 124241.2). Missed by the 09-28 and 10-05 watches; surfaced by NSGA coverage dated Oct. 6.
- Youth tackle football leagues, programs, and coaches must let participants use safety equipment, including soft-shell helmet add-ons. A court can grant an injunction; a prevailing plaintiff gets fees.
- For helmets put into play on or after Jan. 1, 2027, a soft-shell add-on cannot void the warranty or safety certification unless it substantially damages a helmet that was currently certified right before the damage.
- Sources: [Governor's release](https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/09/28/governor-newsom-signs-legislation-protecting-parents-rights-in-youth-football/); [chaptered bill text](https://ct35.capitoltrack.com/25/bill/ViewDoc?doc=%2F%2Fasm%2Fab_0701-0750%2Fab_708_95_C_bill.pdf); [NSGA, Oct. 6, 2026](https://nsga.org/news/california-bill-allows-use-of-add-ons-to-football-helmets/) (Riddell opposed, Certor/Schutt/VICIS supported).
- Site cross-check: `src/content/rules/football.md` and the football body pieces do not state that leagues may ban covers. `guides/football-7v7.md` and `rules/football-7v7.md` mention Guardian Caps only as league-optional 7v7 headgear; no correction needed. No live piece states the old rule as fact.
