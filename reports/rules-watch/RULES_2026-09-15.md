## Data-quality catch: the USSSA "grade exception" row is bad data

The CONTENT_ROADMAP queue has carried a row since 2026-07-18 claiming USSSA caps teams at "3 Grade Exception players," effective 8/12/2026, and it's been re-flagged once already (2026-09-01) because the news file it pointed to was never drafted. This run tried to actually draft it and couldn't stand it up.

I pulled and fully read the official 2026 USSSA National Baseball By-Laws PDF (usssabaseball.org, the primary source AI search kept citing). There is no such rule anywhere in it. Rule 3.00 defines age/grade eligibility cutoffs per division — no numeric cap on grade-exception players. The only "8/12" date in the whole document is the edition's own revision stamp (Revised 8/12/2025), not a rule effective date. Best guess at how this happened: an AI search summary conflated the section header "Age Based with Grade Exceptions Divisions" with an unrelated rule (3.08.B, a 3-player cap on *adding* players to a qualified/frozen postseason roster — nothing to do with grade exceptions) and stitched the two into a claim that doesn't exist in the source.

Logged this as a rejected row in CONTENT_ROADMAP.md with the full explanation, and recommended deleting the original row instead of re-flagging it a third time next week. Nothing to draft here — publishing it would mean shipping a fabricated rule with a fake citation, which fails the voice rubric's sourcing gate before it even gets to voice.
