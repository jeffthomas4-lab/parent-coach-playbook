### Closed open item: USA Swimming House of Delegates R-6 wetsuit vote

**Outcome now published by USA Swimming. R-6 ADOPTED, effective immediately.**

Primary sources fetched this run (both PDFs created Sat Sep 26, 2026, 2:03 pm / 2:10 pm PDT per file metadata):

- 2026 HoD Legislative Actions: https://www.usaswimming.org/docs/default-source/about/rules-and-policies/legislation/2026-house-of-delegates-legislative-actions.pdf
- 2026 Actions Taken by HoD, Summary: https://www.usaswimming.org/docs/default-source/about/rules-and-policies/legislation/2026-house-of-delegates-summary.pdf

Adopted text, rule 701.4.3: only suits complying with World Aquatics open-water specifications may be worn in sanctioned open-water competition. For National and National-qualifying events (i.e. Zone Championships), only wetsuits on the World Aquatics List of Approved Swimwear may be worn. For non-Championship events, wetsuits not on that list but compliant with all of World Aquatics Competition Regulations §6.9 may be worn.

The Rules & Regulations Committee had recommended reject in the pre-vote packet; the House adopted it anyway. Proposer rationale (summary PDF): nearly all listed wetsuits cost $500 or more, and cheaper compliant suits exist in youth sizes.

Close call flagged in the draft's `reviewerNotes`: the proposer's rationale gave "LSC Champs" as an example of a below-Zone meet, but the adopted text says "non-Championship events." The card does not claim LSC championships are covered and tells parents to check the meet announcement.

Other actions from the same meeting (summary PDF):

- R-1 (12&U backstroke turn exception): committed to Age Group Development Committee, not adopted.
- R-2, R-4, R-5, R-8, HK-1, HK-2: adopted; administrative or officials-level.
- R-3 (60-day transfer wait can be waived when the previous club is no longer operating, rule 203.3): adopted, effective immediately. Parent-relevant but narrow. Queued PIPELINE, not drafted.
- R-3A (foreign-federation transfer): committed to Rules & Regulations Committee.
- R-7 (open water, aligned to World Aquatics): adopted/amended, effective immediately. Includes no gels or packaged nutrition carried during the race and a mandatory pre-race safety briefing (no-show without valid reason can mean DQ or yellow card). Queued PIPELINE with R-3, not drafted.
- R-9 (LSC board qualifications): adopted, effective May 1, 2027. Governance only.
- M-1 to M-8: informational, World Aquatics conformity edits already in effect.
- I-1: informational, Maine Swimming and New England Swimming merge into one LSC.

Site cross-check: `src/content/rules/swimming.md` says nothing about wetsuits or the 60-day transfer rule. `src/content/body/open-water-swim-safety.md` discusses wetsuits in general terms only and does not state the old approved-list rule as fact. No live piece needs a correction.
