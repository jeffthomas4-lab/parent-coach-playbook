Two items cleared the bar for a "This Season" draft, both NFHS committee decisions from earlier this year that take effect starting with the season now underway:

1. **NFHS volleyball**: teams can designate up to two liberos per set (only one on court at a time), and the libero's uniform bottom no longer has to match the rest of the team's — only the jersey top does. Approved at the Jan 11-13 committee meeting, effective 2026-27, live now. Verified directly against nfhs.org.
2. **NFHS flag football**: eight rule changes for 2026-27, the sport's first major rules update since most states sanctioned it. Parent-visible ones: a trailing team can elect to keep the ball after scoring and start 4th down at its own 20 instead of kicking off, a fourth field-size option, a hard seven-player-per-side start requirement, and instant replay now limited to state postseason play only. Sourced via MaxPreps, NFL Play Football, and Sports Destination Management — all three describe the same eight changes consistently, but I did not independently pull NFHS's own story for this one, so it's secondary sourcing, not primary. Noted in the file's `reviewerNotes`.
