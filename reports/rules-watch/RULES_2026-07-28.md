## What was drafted

**`src/content/news/ohio-sam-act-athlete-mobility-2026.md`** (draft: true)
Ohio's new Student Athlete Mobility (SAM) Act lets a public high schooler petition to play a sport their own school doesn't offer at a neighboring public school, effective this school year (2026-27). Both superintendents must approve, and OHSAA's own roster minimums still apply. Genuinely new, state-association-level, eligibility-relevant, and imminent — the law takes effect as fall seasons start. Source: Athletic Business (published July 27, 2026, one day before this run), citing OHSAA notification to member schools and Sports Illustrated reporting.

**`src/content/news/nfhs-football-equipment-rules-2026.md`** (draft: true)
NFHS changed two football equipment rules for the 2026 season: mouth/tooth protectors can no longer carry non-functional attachments, and play cards (the QB wristband) can now be worn on the belt instead of only the arm. The announcement itself is from February 4, 2026 — outside the 14-day window — but it's surfaced now under the "imminent deadline from an older decision" rule, since high school football practice and pad-up mouth-guard checks fall within the next 30 days. Not covered anywhere on the site; `nocsae-helmet-standard-football-2026.md` covers helmet certification, a different rule.
