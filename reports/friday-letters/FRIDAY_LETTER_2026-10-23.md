## Lead

Club volleyball tryouts for 14U run in November. High school basketball tryouts land the same month. So in about three weeks, a lot of families will refresh a team page or walk past a gym door and see a list.

We wrote two pieces for both sides of that list. One is for the parent-coach making the cuts. The other is for the parent whose kid's name isn't on it, which is the harder seat.

Our view is plain. A cut done kindly still hurts, but the child leaves with their dignity and a next step. A cut done badly feels like a verdict on the kid (and the AAP's parent site notes that large numbers of kids quit sports before their teens).

And for football families, league Fridays are over. The next game now depends on a bracket win, and the site might be two hours away.

Four new reads below. Plus one from the archive for the night the list goes up.
