# Article refresh standard

This is the canonical contract for refreshing an existing Parent Coach Desk article. It extends `STYLE.md`, `VOICE-RUBRIC.md`, `EDITORIAL_VOICE.md`, `About Me/Anti AI Writing.txt`, `SAFETY_EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md`, `REVIEW.md`, and `strategy/EDITORIAL-CONTENT-LIFECYCLE.md`. When those files set a stricter rule, the stricter rule wins.

The two BabyLoveGrowth imports published on August 1 and 2, 2026 proved that answer-first metadata, comparison sections, source links, and practical checklists can improve search usefulness. They are references, not a length or layout template. A refresh should keep the strongest parts of the original article and add only the structure the reader needs.

## The outcome

A refreshed article should answer the parent's question in the first screen, make the next decision easier, and still sound like Parent Coach Desk. Search improvements come from a clearer answer, better evidence, clean page relationships, and useful coverage. Word count is not the goal.

## Preserve before changing

- Keep the slug, route, original `publishedAt`, phase, topic, sport, age, hero, and core editorial position unless a reviewed correction requires a change.
- Read adjacent articles before editing. Do not create a second answer to the same search intent. Link, narrow, consolidate, or leave the article alone when overlap is stronger than the refresh opportunity.
- Preserve useful first-person texture, dry humor, and specific coaching detail. Do not flatten a human article into a generic explainer.
- Do not add new prices, dates, rules, medical guidance, statistics, product claims, or recruiting outcomes without a current source that supports the exact claim.
- Never add a provider credit, generation credit, or backlink such as `Article generated by BabyLoveGrowth`.

## Search and reader structure

Use the smallest structure that carries the answer.

1. Add a plain-text `bluf` of 30 to 50 words when the article answers a searchable question. It must answer, not preview.
2. Lead the body with the action or decision. Do not repeat the `bluf` word for word.
3. Use two to four sentence-case H2 sections for a focused 600-to-900-word answer, four to eight for a 900-to-1,400-word deep read, and no more than ten for an exceptional cornerstone. A short note can use zero to three. Headings must carry meaning when read alone.
4. Keep paragraphs to three sentences or fewer. Vary sentence length.
5. Add a checklist or numbered sequence only when order matters. Add at most one table for a real comparison with at least three useful rows. A second table requires a distinct purpose in a data-heavy cornerstone. Never add a table, table of contents, `Key Takeaways`, FAQ block, branded `Pro Tip`, or sources section just to imitate a template.
6. Link two to four relevant Parent Coach Desk pages in context. At least one link should move the parent to a next action when a real next action exists. Do not append an unrelated link dump.
7. Cite the primary authority inline for every rule, safety claim, statistic, dated fact, or measurable product claim. A short source list can follow when it helps verification.
8. Use FAQ frontmatter only for natural questions whose answers are already supported in the article. Do not manufacture FAQ variants for schema volume.

## Length by intent

- `format: note`: 250 to 500 words. A note solves one moment or gives one script.
- focused answer or how-to: 600 to 1,000 words.
- decision, comparison, or safety deep read: 900 to 1,400 words.
- true cornerstone: 1,200 to 1,600 words.
- More than 1,600 words requires an explicit reason in the batch report. Split or cut repeated sections before accepting it.

Do not expand a 450-word article to 1,500 words when 650 words answer the question. Do not keep a thin 250-word article when the decision genuinely needs comparisons, evidence, or a usable script.

## Voice and trust

- Apply `EDITORIAL_VOICE.md`: advice first, reason second, example third; plain language; short paragraphs; collective `we`; direct `you`; no em dashes.
- Apply `About Me/Anti AI Writing.txt`: remove banned words, hedging piles, fake suspense, reframe patterns, summary closes, fake-wisdom triplets, title-case headings, bold-every-paragraph formatting, and uniform sentence rhythm.
- Keep one real voice marker where it fits: a fragment, a flat opinion, a parenthetical aside, mild bite, or a specific number or field detail.
- Do not shame families, promise outcomes, tell parents what to feel, or imply that spending more proves commitment.
- Do not name or identify a child, family, recruit, or private individual. Generalize the situation without inventing a case study.

## Safety and sensitive subjects

Medical, injury, mental-health, body-image, abuse-prevention, and return-to-play articles are hard holds unless the refresh satisfies `SAFETY_EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md`.

- Translate current Tier 1 through Tier 5 authority guidance. Parent Coach Desk is not the medical authority.
- Include the required current review/disclaimer block where the safety standard requires it.
- Cite every factual safety claim inline.
- Do not add treatment, dosage, diagnosis, prevention guarantees, or individualized return-to-play advice.
- Do not mark `citationCheckPassed: true` unless every empirical claim is supported.
- Do not publish a category that requires credentialed medical review until written review evidence exists.

If the evidence is unavailable, leave the article unchanged and mark the batch item `held`, with the reason. A held item does not count as one of ten completed refreshes; take the next locked eligible item only through the deterministic queue command.

## Metadata and editorial state

For every completed refresh:

- Keep `publishedAt` unchanged and `draft: false` unchanged.
- Set `updatedAt` to the actual refresh date when the article schema supports it.
- Supply `seoTitle` only when the computed page title would otherwise miss the query or exceed the production title budget. Prefer 25 to 40 raw characters so the rendered title remains useful after the site suffix.
- Supply `seoDescription` as a specific complete sentence between 140 and 160 characters when possible, never over the schema limit.
- Supply accurate `heroAlt` whenever a hero is present.
- Preserve `externalSource` provenance if it already exists. Provenance is not a public provider credit.
- Update the `editorial` review fields honestly. Never set a passed check that was not performed.

## Ten-post batch contract

The locked queue at `reports/editorial/article-refresh-100.json` is the authority for selection and order. It ranks highest-value refresh opportunities from repository evidence because current Search Console data is too sparse for a traffic ranking.

- Work exactly one pending batch of ten at a time.
- Assign each file to one writer. Never let two writers touch the same article.
- Start only from a clean worktree and stop if an assigned file already has an unrelated change.
- Run a cannibalization check before each edit and record the closest adjacent article or `none`.
- Do not replace a held or colliding item by personal choice. Use the queue generator's deterministic next-eligible behavior and record the substitution.
- Complete content checks and a human-readable batch review before committing the batch.
- A scheduled task may stage a batch and produce a report. It may not push, merge, deploy, or publish without the separate approval gate defined in the tracked scheduled-task source.

## Acceptance checks

Every refreshed file must pass all of these checks:

- valid frontmatter and collection schema;
- no `noindex` or `nofollow` directive on the public article path;
- no `Article generated by BabyLoveGrowth` text or equivalent provider credit;
- no mojibake sequences such as `â€`, `Â`, or replacement characters;
- no em dash, banned AI-writing residue, placeholder, or chatbot handoff text;
- no broken internal route introduced by the refresh;
- no affiliate redirect without the required disclosure state and visible disclosure;
- no unsupported or stale factual claim added by the refresh;
- word count and heading count fit the article's intent rather than a global quota;
- `npm run check:content-lengths`, the article-refresh program check, focused tests, and the production build pass.

The batch report must list the ten files, word counts before and after, headings before and after, internal links added or preserved, sources reviewed, cannibalization neighbor, safety/affiliate status, and every hold or correction. Green automation is necessary. It is not editorial approval by itself.
