# Forge Useful Backlink Earning Standard

**Status:** Draft for Jeff approval
**Applies to:** Parent Coach Desk first; reusable for other Field & Forge public websites after product-specific adaptation
**Owner:** Jeff Thomas
**Operating principle:** Earn links because the destination helps the linking site's audience. Link count and third-party domain-rating scores are secondary evidence, never the objective.

## 1. Outcome

This standard turns backlink work into a governed distribution system. It defines what a useful link is, which acquisition methods are acceptable, how opportunities move from research to verified outcome, and how Parent Coach Desk can prove that links create discovery, trust, and useful customer journeys.

The standard does not authorize outreach, publishing, purchases, reciprocal placements, account creation, production changes, or provider settings. Those remain Jeff approval gates.

## 2. Definitions

- **Qualified referring domain:** An independently controlled website whose audience and subject matter overlap the destination page and whose link is editorially defensible.
- **Earned editorial link:** A link chosen by an independent editor, writer, organization, or partner because the destination adds value.
- **Resource-page link:** A link placed in a curated resource list, policy page, toolkit, curriculum, parent guide, or member library.
- **Profile or citation link:** A truthful organization, founder, author, product, or professional profile that identifies the website. It can aid discovery and entity corroboration even when it does not pass ranking credit.
- **Referral-first placement:** A community, social, directory, or platform link pursued mainly because real people may click it. Reddit, Quora, Pinterest, and many profile platforms belong here.
- **Linkable asset:** A page that gives another publisher a concrete reason to cite it: original data, a calculator, a downloadable template, a decision tree, a primary-source digest, or a uniquely useful guide.
- **Deep link:** A link to the exact relevant article, tool, dataset, or action rather than the homepage.
- **Given link:** An outbound link from a Field & Forge site. Given links remain editorial decisions; they are not currency.

## 3. Non-negotiable rules

1. Relevance beats domain rating. A local youth-sports association linking to a parent safety tool is more useful than an unrelated high-rating startup directory.
2. Deep links beat homepage links when the destination solves the referring page's exact problem.
3. Unique qualified domains beat repeated links from the same domain.
4. Referral usefulness and trust matter even when a link is `nofollow` or `ugc`.
5. No bought ranking links, private blog networks, automated directory blasts, comment spam, fake reviews, undisclosed advertorials, mandatory partner links, or keyword-rich signature links.
6. No excessive reciprocal linking. A legitimate partnership may naturally link both ways, but neither link is consideration for the other.
7. Paid, affiliate, advertorial, and user-generated links must use the appropriate relationship treatment. Ordinary editorial citations must not be mislabeled merely to manipulate ranking.
8. No self-serving Wikipedia additions. Disclose conflicts of interest and use talk-page or independent-editor processes when a truly encyclopedic citation exists.
9. No Google Business Profile unless the product meets Google's in-person customer-contact eligibility rule. An online-only publication does not qualify merely because it has a mailing address.
10. No link destination is pitched until it is live, canonical, indexable, accurate, accessible, mobile-usable, internally linked, and free of broken claims or misleading completeness.
11. No health, safety, child-welfare, legal, recruiting, or financial asset is pitched without the repository's applicable editorial and evidence gates.
12. Provider claims, domain-rating metrics, and backlink forecasts are inputs to investigate, not acceptance evidence.

## 4. Opportunity score

Score every candidate out of 100 before outreach.

| Dimension | Points | Test |
|---|---:|---|
| Audience overlap | 20 | Would a meaningful share of the site's readers be Parent Coach Desk readers or product users? |
| Topical relevance | 15 | Does the exact source page naturally belong beside the proposed destination? |
| Editorial independence | 15 | Does the publisher independently control inclusion and maintain visible standards? |
| Asset fit | 15 | Is there a specific live destination that improves the source page? |
| Referral potential | 10 | Could the placement send qualified people even if it passes no ranking credit? |
| Trust and transparency | 10 | Are ownership, authorship, contact, and policies understandable? |
| Durability | 5 | Is the placement likely to remain useful for at least a year? |
| Attainability | 5 | Is there a real submission path, editor, partner, or relationship? |
| Measurability | 5 | Can source URL, destination, date, referral, and conversion be observed? |

Priority bands:

- **P0 — 80 to 100:** Work now after verification and approval.
- **P1 — 65 to 79:** Add to the next asset-matched batch.
- **P2 — 50 to 64:** Conditional; close the asset, identity, or relationship gap first.
- **Defer — below 50:** Do not spend outreach time now.
- **Avoid — risk veto:** Never pursue while the disqualifying condition exists, regardless of score.

Risk vetoes include paid ranking credit, forced reciprocity, automated uncontrolled placement, irrelevant mass placement, concealed sponsorship, fake identity, false business eligibility, scraped or spun content, a malware or deceptive-ad history, or inability to identify where the link will appear.

## 5. Acquisition lanes

### Lane A — Truthful entity and product profiles

Claim only profiles that accurately represent the company, founder, author, public product, open-source project, or professional work. Use consistent name, description, logo, canonical URL, and `sameAs` relationships. These are foundation citations, not a mass-directory campaign.

### Lane B — Resource-page and organization outreach

Find a specific resource page with a visible gap. Offer one exact parent-facing asset as an optional addition. Do not ask for a generic homepage link. Governing bodies, schools, parks departments, PTAs, libraries, nonprofits, clubs, leagues, and medical or safety organizations are strong candidates when the asset fits.

### Lane C — Original data and digital public relations

Publish reproducible research, methodology, date, limitations, downloadable tables, and press-ready findings. Pitch the finding, not the brand. Update on a defined cadence so citations remain accurate.

### Lane D — Expert commentary and interviews

Respond only where Jeff's real experience fits. Provide a concise answer, verifiable credential, non-promotional bio, and the most relevant supporting page. A mention without a link may still improve entity recognition and future discovery.

### Lane E — Guest education and contributed resources

Write for a real audience under the host's editorial control. The article must stand alone and must not exist primarily to carry optimized anchor text. Prefer one contextual citation or author-profile URL over repeated exact-match links.

### Lane F — Partnerships and co-created resources

Co-create webinars, checklists, surveys, seasonal safety kits, coach-parent orientation materials, or research with organizations that already serve the audience. Document responsibilities, editorial control, disclosure, child/privacy boundaries, and link independence.

### Lane G — Community and referral distribution

Participate in Reddit, Quora, Pinterest, relevant forums, newsletters, and social communities by answering the question fully on-platform. Link only when the destination materially expands the answer and the community permits it. Measure engaged visits and conversions rather than presumed link equity.

### Lane H — Controlled syndication

The canonical site publishes first. Where a platform supports canonical import, use it and verify the resulting tag. Otherwise publish a distinct excerpt, summary, chart, or commentary with a source link. Never create a fleet of near-duplicate doorway posts.

## 6. Provider and automated-exchange monitoring

An owner may authorize an automatic backlink provider for monitored current use while evidence is gathered. Record that state as `active_monitored`; do not describe the links as independently earned or assume that provider-reported links are qualified. During active use, verify a current sample against all of the following:

- every giving and receiving domain is disclosed before or immediately after placement;
- every source URL, target URL, anchor, surrounding text, relationship attribute, and placement date is exportable;
- the destination is topically and audience relevant;
- PCD retains editorial control over outbound links placed in its articles;
- reciprocal pairs and network footprints can be identified;
- paid credits do not purchase undisclosed ranking credit;
- placements can be rejected or removed without retaliation;
- the provider supports prompt removal of unsafe, inaccurate, or irrelevant outbound links;
- no child, health, safety, legal, affiliate, or trust content receives an automatic outbound insertion;
- a manual sample passes this standard and Google's current spam policies.

If those conditions cannot be proven, record a `hold_recommended` decision with exact evidence for the owner. Automation may alert and recommend a hold, but it must not silently change provider state. The owner chooses whether to continue, restrict, hold, or disable the exchange. Keep content generation and measurement analytically separate from the provider's exchange feature, and do not call an automatically created link “earned.”

## 7. Linkable-asset acceptance contract

Before outreach, record:

- canonical URL and public status;
- intended audience and problem solved;
- unique value versus the primary sources already available;
- author or responsible editorial entity;
- source registry and fact-check date;
- original data methodology and limitations when applicable;
- accessible HTML alternative for every chart, image, or download;
- required disclosures;
- internal links into and out of the asset;
- proposed source-page types and example targets;
- conversion action that does not obstruct the resource;
- refresh owner and next review date.

## 8. Lifecycle and authority

Use one canonical ledger per product. Required statuses:

`identified -> verified -> asset_gap | pitch_ready -> approved_to_send -> sent -> replied -> landed -> independently_verified`

Terminal or paused statuses:

`declined | dead | removed | avoid | deferred`

Automation may research, score, detect changes, and draft. Jeff approves account claims, provider enrollment, purchases, outreach sends, publication, production changes, and any reciprocal or commercial arrangement.

Each ledger record must contain:

- stable target ID;
- organization and exact source URL;
- segment and geography;
- named contact or documented submission path;
- exact destination asset;
- opportunity score and risk notes;
- proposed pitch angle;
- status and timestamps;
- approval receipt for send;
- landed link URL and relationship attribute when observable;
- referral and conversion evidence;
- last verification date and removal state.

## 9. Outreach contract

Every pitch must answer five questions in under 150 words unless the recipient requests more:

1. Why this specific page or audience?
2. What exact gap did we notice?
3. What exact PCD asset fills it?
4. Why is the asset credible and current?
5. What is the no-pressure next step?

Never mention domain rating, ranking benefit, link exchange, or an expectation that the recipient owes a link. One useful follow-up is allowed unless a relationship warrants otherwise. Honor opt-outs and record them.

## 10. Measurement

Track three layers separately.

### Link evidence

- independently verified live referring URL;
- unique qualified referring domains;
- deep-link share;
- relevant-source share;
- acquired, removed, and recovered links;
- brand mentions without links;
- anchor concentration and suspicious clusters.

### Audience evidence

- referred sessions and engaged sessions;
- newsletter signups;
- tool use or download;
- return visits;
- conversions appropriate to the product journey.

### Search and GEO evidence

- Search Console external-link sample, recognizing that it is not exhaustive;
- impressions, clicks, and query movement for the linked destination;
- indexed status and canonical selection;
- monitored AI prompt mentions and cited source URLs;
- branded-search growth.

Do not attribute ranking or AI-mention changes to one link without stronger evidence. Keep provider metrics, Search Console data, analytics, and independently verified live URLs distinct.

## 11. Portfolio adaptation packet

To reuse this standard on another site, create a product-specific companion containing:

1. audience and conversion journey;
2. current independent referring-domain baseline;
3. existing and proposed linkable assets;
4. forbidden or confidential surfaces;
5. first 25 scored targets;
6. provider and account inventory;
7. product-specific safety and legal gates;
8. 30/60/90-day execution sequence;
9. measurement events and dashboards;
10. owner approval points.

Do not copy PCD targets, pitches, identities, or outbound links into another product. Reuse the system; adapt the evidence and audience.
