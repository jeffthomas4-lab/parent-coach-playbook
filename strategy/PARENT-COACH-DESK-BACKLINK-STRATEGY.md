# Parent Coach Desk Backlink and Earned Distribution Strategy

**Status:** Draft for Jeff approval
**Date:** 2026-08-03
**Authority:** Product-specific companion to `strategy/BACKLINK-EARNING-STANDARD.md`
**Execution owner:** Nora researches, scores, monitors, and drafts; Jeff approves and sends
**Primary audience:** Parents and guardians of youth athletes, with parent-coaches as a distinct high-value segment

## 1. Decision

Parent Coach Desk will pursue the largest possible set of **useful, relevant, independently defensible referring domains**, not the largest raw backlink count. The acquisition engine will combine:

- linkable tools and original data;
- youth-sports resource-page outreach;
- local and national partnerships;
- expert commentary and podcasts;
- truthful entity and product profiles;
- community/referral distribution;
- controlled syndication;
- BabyLoveGrowth-assisted content, technical analysis, GSC, and LLM visibility work.

BabyLoveGrowth's automatic backlink-exchange lane is **ACTIVE — owner-authorized monitored use as of 2026-08-03**. Its current documentation describes automatic giving and receiving of links through a credit exchange. That does not prove every placement is harmful or helpful, so provider links remain a distinct acquisition class and must be inspected rather than counted as independently earned links. Monitoring may recommend a later hold when actual evidence shows irrelevance, unsafe reciprocity, misleading placement, or another stop condition. Jeff retains the decision to continue, restrict, hold, or disable the lane and must separately approve any purchase of additional backlink credits.

## 3. Success definition

### First 90 days after approval

- 10 independently controlled, live, relevant referring domains verified outside PCD's own portfolio;
- at least 6 links point to exact tools, studies, or guides rather than the homepage;
- at least 3 distinct acquisition lanes land one qualified link each;
- at least 2 referring domains send an engaged visit, signup, tool use, or other measurable audience action;
- zero paid ranking links, undisclosed reciprocal arrangements, or automated placements accepted without the provider gate;
- every sent pitch and landed result is represented in the canonical ledger.

### Six months

- 25 qualified independent referring domains;
- 5 durable linkable assets with assigned refresh owners;
- 3 repeatable partner or media relationships;
- measurable branded-search, referral, newsletter, tool-use, or qualified product-journey growth;
- Search Console, analytics, and BabyLoveGrowth metrics reconciled without treating any one source as complete.

### Twelve months

- 50 qualified independent referring domains across governing bodies, education/local organizations, media, community resources, and product/entity profiles;
- no single platform or provider supplies more than 25% of qualified referring domains;
- the asset and outreach system is packaged for a product-specific SightSmash adaptation.

These are operating targets, not ranking guarantees.

## 5. Linkable asset roadmap

### Ready or nearly ready from existing repository evidence

1. **Youth sports cost calculator** — pitch to parent media, financial-wellness resources, clubs, PTAs, libraries, and journalists covering family costs.
2. **Parent-coach scripts library** — pitch to coach education programs, school athletics pages, leagues, and parent newsletters.
3. **Season calendars and age pathways** — pitch to clubs, rec departments, new-parent orientation pages, and libraries.
4. **Sport rules explained** — pitch as plain-language companions to governing-body rulebooks, not substitutes for them.
5. **Heat, AED, concussion, allergy, bullying, and travel-safety explainers** — pitch only after current primary-source and editorial checks pass.
6. **Adaptive and Unified Sports guides** — pitch to inclusion organizations, school districts, and community recreation programs.
7. **Camp and organization directory** — pitch only where the surfaced records meet the repository's accuracy and index-worthiness thresholds.

### Build as new citation magnets

1. **2026 Youth Sports Family Cost Index.** Aggregate transparent, reproducible category and sport ranges; publish methodology, regional limitations, downloadable CSV, and update schedule.
2. **Parent Pulse survey.** A small but methodologically honest recurring survey on costs, playing time, travel burden, burnout, and communication. Never collect child-sensitive data.
3. **Coach-parent conversation card pack.** Printable and accessible cards for tryouts, cuts, injuries, playing time, bad games, and coach contact.
4. **Youth sports safety readiness matrix.** A primary-source-linked matrix for heat, lightning, AED, concussion, allergy, emergency contacts, and transportation. Clearly separate organization rules from PCD explanation.
5. **First-season launch kit.** Calendar, budget worksheet, equipment questions, communication scripts, and parent orientation checklist.
6. **Annual rules-change digest.** A dated, sport-by-sport digest that links to the actual governing rule source and states jurisdiction and effective season.
7. **Travel-team true-cost worksheet.** Calculator plus anonymized scenario analysis, never fabricated nationwide averages.
8. **Youth-sports resource embed kit.** Approved logo, one-sentence description, screenshot, printable PDF, and plain link snippets for resource managers. No hidden, sitewide, or keyword-stuffed widget links.

## 6. BabyLoveGrowth operating lane

### Use now

- preserve the established audience description and the existing 32-topic queue;
- use the 30-day plan as a demand and coverage input, not as automatic editorial authority;
- generate answer-first drafts with question-shaped headings, concise definitions, tables where useful, and primary citations;
- require every article to serve a real PCD reader and fit an existing topic cluster;
- connect each approved article to one existing or proposed linkable asset;
- use technical-audit findings as proposals to reconcile with repository truth;
- compare BabyLoveGrowth GSC and LLM visibility reports with Search Console and first-party analytics;
- use its Reddit discovery only to find relevant conversations; a human must review and make a genuinely useful contribution before any link is posted;
- keep provider branding or generation-credit backlinks out of PCD article bodies, consistent with `strategy/ARTICLE-REFRESH-STANDARD.md`.

### Article acceptance additions for SEO and GEO

Every BabyLoveGrowth article must have:

- a direct answer in the opening section;
- a clear PCD-specific audience and decision;
- at least one piece of original PCD value beyond source summarization;
- primary sources for material factual claims;
- visible fact-check or updated date when facts can change;
- no unsupported statistics, invented experts, fake quotations, or implied clinical/legal authority;
- a named author or honest editorial-entity treatment consistent with the current reveal state;
- accessible lists, tables, images, and downloads;
- internal links chosen for the reader journey;
- no automatic outgoing commercial, exchange, or provider-credit link;
- a destination role: `answer`, `supporting_cluster`, `linkable_asset`, `comparison`, or `timely_update`;
- a post-publish check for HTTP 200, title, canonical, indexability, structured data, internal links, and mobile rendering.

### Automatic backlink exchange: active monitored lane

While the exchange remains active:

1. Export every link PCD has given and received, including source URL, target URL, anchor, surrounding text, date, domain, and relationship attribute.
2. Identify direct and indirect reciprocal pairs.
3. Score each domain and exact placement against the shared standard.
4. Inspect at least 20 placements or all placements when fewer than 20 exist.
5. Confirm whether PCD can pre-approve, reject, remove, or exclude categories and URLs.
6. Confirm whether health, safety, child-welfare, legal, affiliate, and trust articles can be excluded.
7. Confirm whether purchased credits cause additional unqualified followed links.
8. Compare the placement pattern with Google's current link-spam examples.
9. Record the current state as `active_monitored` and classify provider-network links separately from independent links.
10. Review the lane monthly for the first 90 days and recommend one of: `continue_monitored`, `restrict`, `hold`, or `disable`.
11. Do not change provider state automatically. Present the evidence and recommendation to Jeff.

No purchased backlink credits are authorized by this document.

## 8. The supplied 75-site list: decision map

The supplied domain-rating numbers are not used as acceptance evidence. Platform fit and current eligibility must be verified at execution time.

### Use now when the profile or content is real

- **GitHub and GitHub Pages:** publish real public methodology, datasets, calculators, or documentation; never doorway pages.
- **Crunchbase:** truthful company/entity profile.
- **Pinterest:** referral-first distribution for checklists, tools, calendars, scripts, and sport-season content.
- **Medium:** import selected existing articles so the original canonical is retained, or publish distinct commentary.
- **Substack:** newsletter and excerpt distribution; avoid cloning the full corpus without a verified canonical treatment.
- **Reddit and Quora:** answer-first community participation; link only when it genuinely extends the answer.
- **Product Hunt:** launch a live, polished digital tool or meaningful product release, not the editorial homepage.
- **Indie Hackers:** founder/build story and transparent learning, not youth-sports keyword promotion.
- **Hashnode and Dev.to:** only for genuine technical articles about the public implementation, data methodology, accessibility, or open-source tools.

### Conditional on a real product or milestone

- **Chrome Web Store:** only if PCD ships a useful browser extension.
- **TrustRadius, PeerSpot:** after a reviewable B2B product and real customers exist; never seed reviews.
- **AlternativeTo, Alternative Me, SaaSHub:** when PCD has a public software/tool category and comparable alternatives.
- **SourceForge, OSS Gallery:** only for a maintained open-source or downloadable software artifact.
- **Gumroad:** only for a real paid or free downloadable product.
- **Goodreads:** when a real published book or author page exists.
- **BetaList, TinyLaunch, Tiny Startups, Indie Page, WIP, Milestones:** only around a genuine product launch with an audience fit.
- **StackShare:** when there is a truthful public technology stack story worth maintaining.
- **Toolify AI:** only when a working AI capability is publicly available and accurately described.
- **TrustMRR, Latka, Founder Reports, Revenue Memo, Boring Cash Cow, Micro Founder, Failory, Starter Story:** only after verifiable revenue, failure, or growth evidence supports the outlet's format.
- **GrowthMentor, FounderPass, Builder Society, SaaS Club:** founder community participation when there is a real contribution beyond profile creation.
- **MakerPad:** verify current ownership, activity, and submission path before investing work.

### Earned editorial targets; not “submit a backlink” sites

- **Forbes, YourStory, HackerNoon, Foundr, The Hustle, Smashing Magazine, TechCrunch, VentureBeat, First Round Review, AppSumo Blog, SaaStr, Entrepreneur, Niche Pursuits, Mixergy, My First Million, Indie Bites:** pitch only a genuinely newsworthy story, original dataset, useful expert contribution, or relevant product milestone.
- **DZone:** technical contributed content only when PCD has an engineering lesson its readers would value.
- **SEO Wins and Indie Niche:** verify present editorial quality and fit before pitching any case study.

### Referral-only or generally low priority for PCD

- **Imgur, Flickr, Pixabay, Pexels:** use only for original, rights-cleared visual assets with truthful attribution and a genuine portfolio/resource purpose. Do not upload stock-like images for profile links.
- **HubPages, Vocal Media, Blogger:** low-priority syndication; Medium's canonical import or owned newsletter excerpts are cleaner first choices.
- **Fandom:** only if an independent community has an actual encyclopedic need; no promotional page creation.
- **WikiHow:** contribute expertise only through its editorial rules; do not force a self-citation.

### Do not pursue under current PCD facts

- **Google Business Profile and Yelp:** PCD appears to be an online publication/product; Google explicitly excludes online-only businesses. Revisit only if a genuine customer-facing local operation exists.
- **Privacy Tools:** no topical fit unless PCD ships a meaningful privacy tool.
- **Wikipedia:** no self-created brand article or self-placed link. Independent notability and editor judgment must come first.

6. suspicious or unreviewed provider placements.

## 11. Stop conditions

Pause a lane or campaign when:

- two placements show obvious irrelevance, spun context, or undisclosed reciprocity;
- a provider cannot export exact given and received links;
- outreach complaints or opt-outs are not honored;
- a destination is inaccurate, stale, unavailable, or fails its safety gate;
- the same anchor or destination pattern becomes unnaturally concentrated;
- a platform requires payment for followed ranking credit;
- traffic is automated, misleading, or has no plausible human audience;
- a campaign creates links but no useful discovery and consumes more effort than higher-fit alternatives.
